builds the FAQ from the table: five questions on the current page, three on an archive (two for an

structured data (`renderFaqSchema`: the `scf-faq-data` island embedded, an FAQPage standalone) both
read that one list. Every figure an answer quotes is read from the code that applies it: scoring from
`scoring/house-rules.ts`, blend weights from `fpa/blend.ts` `currentSeasonWeight()`, the window from

values (teams, figures, ranks) are quoted.

**Plugin (`statchasers-page-identity.php`).** FPA joins WEB-021 as family `fpa`: Rank Math's global

## Final question list

### Current route (5)

1. What does Fantasy Points Allowed (FPA) measure?
   Per game the defense played: points scored against it by a position, added up and divided by its
   games; OFF = the four positions added. Rank 1 = most allowed (softest), 32 = fewest; ties
   alphabetical by team abbreviation (`rankFpaValues`).
2. How does the scoring format change FPA?
   Only receptions differ: shared 0.04/pass yd, 4/pass TD, -1/INT, 0.1/rush or rec yd, 6/rush or rec
   TD, 2/two-point, -2/fumble lost; Half-PPR +0.5 per reception, PPR +1. Opens in PPR.
3. What is the difference between raw FPA and adjusted FPA (aFPA)?
   adj = raw - (average over the games faced of each opponent's own points per game at the position
   - league average of offenses' points per game at the position), as in `computeFpaTable`.
4. How do games played, byes and last season affect the figure?
   Per game played, so byes cost nothing; Games column = games the figure covers. Blend: 2026 weight
   25/40/60/80% after weeks 1-4, +2 points a week to 90%; once a 12th week of games is in, 2025 drops
   out and the last 10 weeks of 2026 games are read. Then "The current table: {methodology}".
5. When is the FPA table updated?
   Recalculated after games, from published player stats; the line under the heading gives the date,
   the last complete week, and any games counted from a week in progress. No promised cadence.

### /2025/ archive (3)

1. What does the 2025 Fantasy Points Allowed table cover?
   Complete regular season, Weeks 1-17 (verified: `ARCHIVE_MAX_WEEK = 17`, `readFpaArchiveTable`),
   all 32 defenses; Week 18 left out; 2025 data only, no blend; final figures.
2. How are the 2025 figures and ranks calculated?
   Same arithmetic without the blend: per game played in Weeks 1-17 (byes not games), aFPA removes
   offense strength, rank 1 = most, ties by abbreviation.
3. How is the 2025 season used in the 2026 table?
   It is the baseline: 2026 preseason = 2025 in full; 2026 games gain weight weekly; once a 12th week
   of 2026 games is in, 2025 drops out.

Decision on the archive: give it its own FAQ rather than none. The October choice was right about
the old FAQ (19 general answers at a second address would have been duplicate copy and duplicate
schema). These three are about what only that page has: its coverage, that it is unblended and
final, and its role as next season's baseline. Not one question or answer is shared with the
current page (asserted in `tests/fpa.test.mjs`), and nothing mentions a current week or an update.
An archive older than the baseline season gets only the first two questions.

## Structured data matches visible text

`scripts/fpa-faq-schema-check.mjs <file-or-url>...` parses rendered HTML, takes the visible FAQ from
the `.scf-faq-item` folds and the structured FAQ from ld+json (FAQPage node or a Rank Math page node
typed FAQPage) or the `scf-faq-data` island, and fails on any difference in count, order, question
or answer text, on more than one FAQ carrier, or on an em dash. Run against the local render:

```
local /fpa (standalone)            OK - 5 questions, identical in ld+json
local /fpa?season=2025             OK - 3 questions, identical in ld+json
local /embed/fpa                   OK - 5 questions, identical in scf-faq-data island
local /embed/fpa?season=2025       OK - 3 questions, identical in scf-faq-data island
WP model, current (real fragment)  OK - 5 questions, identical in ld+json .../#webpage
WP model, /2025/ (real fragment)   OK - 3 questions, identical in ld+json .../2025/#webpage
live before (current)              OK - 19 questions, identical in ld+json (no @id)
live before (/2025/)               no FAQ shown and none in structured data
negative control (one word edited) FAIL, as it should
```

The WP model rows come from the page-identity smoke child fed the real local fragment:
`STATCHASERS_SMOKE_GROUP=pageid STATCHASERS_SMOKE_ROUTE=fpa:hub STATCHASERS_FPA_FRAGMENT=<embed.html> STATCHASERS_FPA_RENDER_OUT=<out.html> php tests/smoke.php`
(`fpa:2025` for the archive). It writes the printed table plus the graph as WordPress would carry them.

## Finding outside this scope: the live table is not 90/10

The brief expected the current table to be a 90/10 2026/2025 blend at Week 11. `/api/status` on
2026-11-27 reports `weekNumber 12, dataMode week_12`: the 2026-11-26 ingest read the one Week 12
game played (GB at LAR), so `weeksPlayed` = 12 distinct weeks, `currentSeasonWeight(12)` = 1, and
the table is the rolling ten-week window of 2026 only (weeks 3-12, with week 12 a single game),
while the title and Updated line say "Weeks 1-11". The FAQ states what is applied (the methodology
sentence), so it is accurate; whether a single Thursday game should switch the method and shift the
window is a data question for a separate item. Titles were not touched.
