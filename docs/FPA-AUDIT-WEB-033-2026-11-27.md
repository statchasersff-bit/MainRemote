The trace rebuilt the table with the app's own `computeFpaTable`, `blendWithCurrent` and
`rankFpaValues` (src/lib/fpa/math.ts). It used the stored rows, 2026 weeks 1-11 plus the 2025
baseline. The settings were the ones the audit saw on 11-25:

| Setting | Value |

| Blend | `currentSeasonWeight(11)` = 0.90, so 10% 2025 full season + 90% 2026 weeks 1-11 |

| MIN | PPR | 20.056 | 18.140 (10, bye) | **18.33** | **29** |

The FPA page, the game-log Difficulty cell and the upcoming strip print that rank, from the same
`getFpaTable({format, view: "raw"})` read:
- the FPA page renders `table.rows[].byPos.RB.rank`;
- the game log reads it through `fpaOppRanks` -> `attachOppRanks`;
- the strip reads it through `matchupTable` -> `buildUpcoming`.

**Bare page vs `?format=ppr`.** On 11-25 the bare FPA page was Half-PPR, so it showed ARI 11th,
while `?format=ppr` showed 13th, like the game log. Since 1.10.74 the bare page is PPR. Live today,
all 32 RB cells on the bare page equal `?format=ppr`, rank for rank and figure for figure.

**Bye and uneven games played (the audit's hypothesis): not the cause.**
- Per game divides by the games each defense actually played. Through Week 11, 26 defenses had
  10 games and 6 had 11 (ARI, BAL, DAL, IND, LV, NYJ).
- ARI (11 games) and MIN (10) are both divided correctly.
- Both surfaces read the same per-game figure, so games played cannot make them disagree.
- The 11-26 fallback fix (`buildOppRanks`, seasons before the archive) uses the same per-game
  rule.

| Surface | Format named | Direction | Bands |
|---|---|---|---|
| FPA page | caption "PPR scoring, raw view"; Scoring control | "Nth most points allowed" | Softest 1-6 / Soft 7-12 / Middle of the pack 13-20 / Tough 21-26 / Toughest 27-32 (`matchupTier`) |

| Upcoming Matchups strip | "in PPR: 1st allows the most, 32nd the fewest" | same | same three words |
| Header chip | "3rd vs. RBs, favorable" | same | same, lowercase |

- The cut points are the same on every player-page surface and on the FPA page (12/13 and
  20/21). The FPA page splits each end band into two tiers.
- Before this branch, the three player-page band vocabularies were three hand-typed maps that
  happened to match: `DIFFICULTY_WORD` and `BAND_READ` in player-render.ts, and `CHIP_WORD` in
  header.ts.
- Fixed here: all three now derive from `BAND_WORDS` (fpa/matchup-bands.ts), which existed but was
  unused. The Game Log note's "Favorable 1-12, Neutral 13-20, Tough 21-32." is built from
  `SOFT_MAX_RANK` and `TOUGH_MIN_RANK`.
- No visible text changes.

**Matchup pages: flagged, not changed.** They use the same FPA rank (per format) with 10/23 cuts:
- the Fantasy Football Impact card (preview-fantasy.ts: "Favorable Matchup" 1-10, "Tough Matchup"
  23-32);
- the Matchup Leverage data (`chipFor`).

Those are stricter selection thresholds, so a matchup-page "Favorable" is always Favorable on the
player page. One real contradiction remains: preview.ts `snapshotNote` says *"Neither defense is
soft at any position"* when the softest spot ranks 11th or 12th. The FPA page calls those ranks
"Soft (7-12)". That sentence is matchup FAQ answer text, so it needs mak's OK
(seo-changes-need-approval). The fix would be `soft.rank <= SOFT_MAX_RANK` (or new wording).

## Observation (not WEB-033, flagged for mak): the Week 12 jump

From `weeksPlayed >= 12` the table switches to a rolling ten-week window, 2026 only
(`currentWindowWeeks`, `currentSeasonWeight(12) = 1`).

`weeksPlayed` counts distinct weeks with any row, so one Thursday game of Week 12 flips it:
- weeks 1-2 drop out for every defense;
- the 2025 share goes from 10% to 0;
- only the clubs that played that Thursday gain a game.

Live on 11-26, after GB at LAR, most defenses average 8 or 9 games instead of 10 or 11. The ranks
moved sharply overnight, for example:
- BUF 12 -> 20;
- CAR 1 -> 7;
- CHI 23 -> 26;
- NE 15 -> 10.

The player-page cutoff label says "through Week 11 and 1 Week 12 game". It names the end of the
window, not its start (Week 3). All surfaces still agree with each other.

An auditor comparing against the 11-25 numbers will see "new" differences that are this
methodology step, not a ranking bug. Counting only complete weeks in `weeksPlayed`, or saying
"Weeks 3-11 and ..." in the label, is a methodology/wording call for mak. Nothing was changed.
