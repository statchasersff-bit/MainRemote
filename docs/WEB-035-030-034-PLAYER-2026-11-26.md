## What changed

- `src/lib/player/season-phase.ts`: `publishedRank(data)` returns `{rank, basis: "ppg" | "total"}` and `rankBasisWords()`; `currentRank()` delegates to it. Overview (`overview.ts`) and Outlook (`outlook-tab.ts`) label every printed rank from `basis`.
- Share comparisons use ONE field, the Outlook's usage field (`outlook-context.ts playerUsage`), now with a per-metric percentile (`pct`, the Stats card's rule: share of the other qualified players he is above) and `fieldNote` ("6+ games and 1.5+ targets per team game"; RB "5+ weighted opportunities", QB "12+ dropbacks"):
  - Stats card (`stats-tab-data.ts withUsageField`): Target share, Snap share and Air-yards share rows take the usage field's rank and percentile; the footnote says so beside the card's own pool. A player the field leaves out is "Not ranked" on those rows. An unqualified card stays unranked.
  - Overview meters (`overview-data.ts metersFromCard`): this season's meters ARE the Stats card's readings (same figure, pool and percentile); the footnote names both fields. RB meters are now YACO per carry, explosive run rate, target share, points per opportunity (the card has no yards-per-carry row); QB meters swap fantasy points per play for EPA per play. Past seasons without a card keep the old pool and footnote.
  - Outlook drivers: the Downfield role row takes the usage field's air-yards share rank; the drivers lede names the field and its rule when every row is in it.
- IR / unavailable (`overview.ts noGamesLead`, `outlook-tab.ts`, `faq-pools.ts`): in season with no game, the Overview leads with "{Name} has not played in 2026." plus the status; a sidelined player gets no preseason take, a healthy one gets it dated ("From our preseason outlook (June 2026): ..."). On a reserve list the Outlook schedule verdict reads "He is on injured reserve, so these matchups matter only once he is activated.", the playoff read and the schedule/playoff FAQs go, the Overview matchups FAQ goes, and the Outlook upside FAQ drops the soft schedule and "weekly floor" for any sidelined player.
- TD labels: "receiving TD" / "receiving touchdowns" (WR/TE), "rush TD" / "rushing touchdowns" (RB stops rail and Bio sentence), "pass TD" (QB stops rail).
