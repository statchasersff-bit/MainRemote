## 1. Truncated play-by-play wording

**Fixture.** Ravens at Panthers, 2026 Week 11 (stored data), with the play-by-play file cut right after Ryan
Fitzgerald's 37-yard field goal at 1:47 of the fourth quarter (33-35). The official final is 33-35, so the
score on the last available play matches the final. In the real game a Baltimore drive (Dodson's interception)
and a Carolina kneel-down followed. Before this change, `endsGameAt` (nothing after the play in the file)
and `isFinalScore` (the final agrees) were both true.

| Surface (component) | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Recap body (`recap-narrative.ts` engine, used when the stored prose's hash no longer matches) | "Ryan Fitzgerald made a 37-yard field goal with 1:47 left, to win it 35–33 on the final play." | "Ryan Fitzgerald made a 37-yard field goal with 1:47 left, to put Carolina ahead 35–33 for good." |
| How the Game Swung (`recap-overview.ts`) | "Fitzgerald makes the game-winning field goal." | "Fitzgerald makes the go-ahead field goal." |
| FAQ (`recap-body.ts`) | "Ryan Fitzgerald's 37-yard field goal on the final play then won it." | "Ryan Fitzgerald's 37-yard field goal then put them ahead for good." |
| Scoring summary, drive label, WP chart | no ending claim (clock 1:47) | unchanged |

"For good" is the box score's scoring sequence: it adds up to the final and leaves Carolina ahead after every
later score (`leadHeldToFinal`, b8d454a).

**Rule.** A score ends the game only when the file has nothing after it AND ESPN's drive chart, an
independent record, agrees (`play-facts.ts` `espnGameEnding` / `withGameEnding` / `endsGameConfirmed`):

- the game's last drive is the scorer's touchdown or field goal, or the defense's touchdown on it, and
- in regulation that drive ran to 0:00 (start clock minus time elapsed). An overtime drive ends on the score.

Without a drive chart, or with one that disagrees, no surface says "final play" or "game-winning", and the
snap clock stays.

**Genuine walk-off.** Packers at Lions, Week 7. ESPN's last drive is DET, starting Q4 3:21 with 3:21 elapsed,
so it ended at 0:00; result FG. The play-by-play has nothing snapped or kicked after Bates' kick (snap 0:01),
and the final is 17-20. The page prints "Bates' 22-yard field goal on the final play won it." (FAQ) and
"Bates makes the game-winning field goal." with "Q4 0:01" (swing card). The recap says "with one second
left", and the scoring summary shows Q4 · 0:01. No 0:00 appears anywhere on the page.

**Real data unchanged.** All 163 final 2026 game pages render byte-identical text before and after (local
e96626e vs this branch, full page text md5). That covers the 16 genuine walk-offs and the Giants' pick-six as
time expired at Arizona (Week 4); every stored recap is still served.

**Tests** (`tests/recap-accuracy.test.mjs`, 33 pass):
- `espnGameEnding` unit cases.
- The cut Ravens-Panthers file: rendered Overview and FAQ copy is neutral.
- Packers-Lions with the chart: walk-off wording, 0:01 everywhere.
- Packers-Lions without a chart, or with a contradictory one: neutral.
