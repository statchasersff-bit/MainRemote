One resolver, `gameAvailability` (matchup/availability.ts), feeds the preview Injury Watch. Through `currentStatus` (player/current-status.ts) it also feeds the player header, the Outlook, the Injury tab and the FAQ. Each result carries:

- season and week
- source: `nfl-report` / `sleeper` / `reserve` / `none`
- report state (`missing` / `pending` / `final`) and the report's fetch time

A practice line is kept apart from the game status. A reserve list is a `reserve` kind, never counted as "designated". A week with no report reads "Game designation pending", never "no designation" or "Active".

The open SEO item from 11-24b is already closed on main by 7cfcb65. The Injury tab meta, og and twitter descriptions (`injuryAnswer().lead`) say "Game designation pending for Week N" before the report is final, and "No game designation" after. Nothing more was needed.

## Current finding: where the remaining wrong numbers came from

Every games-missed figure on Isiah Pacheco's seven tabs was collected, locally and on production (read-only, 11-26). The season total, the open IR stint, the hub "Season Status", the FAQ answers and the region record all say **10**.

The one figure that did not was the **Durability Profile's season grid**:

- It drew **5** red boxes for 2026, with no bye, and 13 boxes reading "beyond this season's game count".
- Production shows the same. Bo Melton's 2026 row on production draws 6 games played; he has played 10.

Root cause: the card reads `data/injury-impact/gridiron.json`. That file is the injury-impact research pack, generated 2026-10-20 "through week 6". Pacheco's 2026 entry there is `"s": [0,5]`, `"w": "XXXXX"`. Only that repository's pipeline repacks it; `refresh.mjs` does not touch it.

The 11-24 work patched the pack's figures in three places:

- the tile total
- the open episode
- the region total

It did not patch the grid, the career availability rate, or any current-season episode that was not open. That leaves a second, broader class of errors on every player hurt after Week 6:

### Pending-game claims (the "missing result read as confirmation" case)

Bo Melton is on the official Week 12 report as Questionable (thigh), read 11-25 17:08Z. The game had not been played in the stored data. Before this change:

- The history said "Listed on the injury report with a quad injury from Week 12 and played through it without missing time".
- The latest-injury card said "Week 12, 2026, played through".
- The durability record said "Listed Questionable but didn't miss a game".

The source is `buildInjuryHistory.mjs`, which writes "played through" for a report week before its game. Now:

- A current-season entry whose report weeks are all games still to come is marked `gameToCome` at render time (player-data.ts `withGamesToCome`, rule `gameToCome()` in games-missed.ts).
- It reads "Game not played yet" in the history chip.
- It reads "game not played yet" on the latest-injury card.
- It reads "Listed Questionable for Week 12; that game has not been played yet." in the durability record, and "game to come" on the figure label and marker.

Also fixed:

- **Body part.** The Injury tab said "questionable for Week 12 with an injury" (Sleeper had no body part) beside the report's Thigh. An `nfl-report` designation now carries the report's injury (`Availability.injury`).
- **"Last played".** The Injury tab said "Last played Week 9" beside "He has played every game in 2026". Melton's Week 10 was special teams only: `gp` 1, 0 offensive snaps. "Last played" and the FAQ's played count now use the season count's played rule (games-missed.ts).

## What each counter measures (labels on the page)

| Counter | Where | Measures | Period label |
|---|---|---|---|
| "2026 games missed" | Injury tab tile, hub Season Status, FAQ | His club's completed regular-season games inside an injury entry's window (games-missed.ts). Byes skipped. A not-played game with no injury record is counted apart, never as injury. Weeks before a mid-season signing are not charged | "2026" / "this season" |
| "Latest injury ... N games missed" | Injury tab tile, history | One stint: its window. Open stints run to the last completed game | "Week 1, 2026" / "so far" |
| History rows | Injury tab | Each stored entry, regular-season games | "Games missed are regular-season games" |
| Region record and pill | Durability card | Sum of the region's episodes. Current season from the same windows | Seasons on each row |
| "Games Missed (over N seasons)" | Durability tile | Pack seasons, the current one replaced by the season count | "over N seasons", plus ", playoffs included" when the pack counts playoff games (289 players) |
| Grid | Durability card | One box per week. The current season from `SeasonAvailability.weekCodes`: P played, X injury, - other, B bye, N not yet on a club | Season rows. "not played yet" for weeks to come |
| "Games appeared in" | Stats tab | Playing time, not health | Already labeled "2022 through 2026 Week 11 ... measures playing time, not health" |

The pack's durability score and tier stay as packed. They rank the player against the whole 1,652-player set, which is scored as of the pack. That is noted in the code.
