## What changed

- `src/lib/player-render.ts`: on the profile, the Stats, Game Log, Outlook, Injury, News and Bio panels carry their body and footer only; the peers rail and the FAQ print once, in the Overview, and the FAQ island (the FAQPage WordPress builds) is the Overview's questions only. Each tab's own address (`/nfl/players/<slug>/<tab>/`) is unchanged: rail, FAQ and FAQPage of its own. In-place tab switching on the profile still works (same panels, same client code); a reader who switches there sees the tab without its rail and FAQ.
- `src/lib/player/faq-engine.ts`: `FAQ_MAX` 15 -> 10 on every tab (pool order is priority; never forced).
- `src/lib/player/bio.ts`: Bio questions that only restate another answer are gone: position (number and team answers name it), draft round and draft pick (the draft answer gives both), birthday (the age answer), entry year (the experience answer). A drafted veteran now has 10.
- `src/lib/player/news-tab.ts`: the hidden "No updates of this type yet this season." line is no longer printed.
- `src/lib/player/overview.ts` + `public/player.css`: the Season Stats block keeps its position-appropriate headline numbers (QB passing line, RB rushing + receiving, WR/TE receiving + rushing, FPTS) and its link is now "Full {Name} stats" (`data-cta="full-stats-overview"`), shown at every width including phones.
- `public/player.css` (appended block "Readable labels"): a 12 px floor for the Overview's and the peers rail's text labels at every width. Not changed: the bar chart's in-plot values and week marks, and the header (mak, 11-15).
